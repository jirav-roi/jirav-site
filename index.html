The problem

Your best work rests on one person's spreadsheet

Advisory and FP&A are the most profitable part of a practice. Yet most firms build them on individual accountants' Excel models. It works until it doesn't.

1

When people leave, so do the models

The forecast, the know-how and often the client relationship sit with whoever built the file. When they resign, the firm starts again, or loses the client.

2

Every client gets a different model

Different layouts, different logic, different quality. Reviews take longer, juniors can't pick it up, and the client experience depends on who's on the job.

3

Growth depends on staff you can't find

If each new advisory client needs another hard-to-hire senior to build and run a bespoke model, growth stops at the size of your team.

You'd never build a house without solid foundations. In a practice, the foundation is your data. Excel feels free, and every good accountant is master of their own universe. But it's their universe, not yours.

How it works

An ERP for advisory, built on Xero

Jirav becomes your firm's standard FP&A platform: one way to budget, forecast and report for every advisory client, owned by the firm.

  1. Connect Xero

    Each client's Jirav connects to their own Xero file, so actuals flow in automatically. No exports, no copy-paste. QuickBooks Online and NetSuite are supported too.

  2. Standardise on one model

    Build your firm's driver-based template once and reuse it for every client: three-way forecasts, budgets and scenarios with the same logic every time.

  3. Deliver under your brand

    Board packs, budget vs actual and rolling cash forecasts, white-labelled for your firm. Your team delivers them, or ours does it for you.

  • The firm owns the data and the IP. Models live on the firm's platform, not on someone's laptop.
  • Consistent delivery. Every client gets the same standard, whoever is on the job.
  • Easier to delegate. A shared template means more of your team can do the work.
  • Keep Excel for the thinking. Jirav is the system of record, not a ban on spreadsheets.

FAQ

Questions firms ask us

Who owns the data and the models?

Your firm does. Each client's Jirav sits on the firm's platform, built from the firm's templates. If a staff member leaves, the models, history and know-how stay with you.

How does Jirav work with Xero?

Jirav is a Xero marketplace partner. Each client's Jirav connects to their own Xero file and syncs actuals automatically, so forecasts and reports run on current numbers rather than last month's export.

Do we have to give up Excel?

No. Keep Excel for ad hoc thinking. The point is that the firm's core advisory models live in one standard platform the firm owns, not in individual files.

What does "white-labelled" mean in practice?

Board packs and reports go out under your firm's brand. On Platform + Service, our team works as part of yours. You keep the client relationship and all billing goes through your firm.

What's in the monthly board pack?

Commentary, budget vs actual and a rolling cash forecast, built on the client's live Xero data and presented under your brand. Platform + Service also includes a monthly 1-hour session with the client.

How does the AI commentary work?

Released in September 2026, Jirav drafts commentary inside the board pack from the client's actual numbers. A person on your team (or ours) reviews and edits it before it's sent.

Are there minimums or set-up fees?

There's no minimum number of clients. Your first client's onboarding (worth A$2,000) and first month are free. We'll walk you through terms for further clients on a call.

Who are JIRAV APAC?
